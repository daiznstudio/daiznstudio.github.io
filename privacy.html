This policy explains what personal information daizn studio collects when you visit this website or get in touch with us, why we collect it, and what you can ask us to do with it. We keep it short because we collect very little.

01Who we are

daizn studio is a small design, social media and software studio. In this policy, "we", "us" and "our" mean daizn studio and its team members.

02What we collect

Information you choose to send us

When you press "Submit message" on a contact form, your name, email address and message are sent to us through Web3Forms, a form delivery service that passes them on to our studio inbox. When you use the form, or when you email or message us directly, we receive:

  • your name and email address;
  • the content of your message, along with any files or details you choose to include;
  • any other details you share while we talk about a project, such as your company name or phone number.

Information collected automatically

We do not use analytics, advertising trackers or cookies on this website. Like almost every website, the server that hosts it may keep standard technical logs, such as your IP address, browser type and the pages requested, so that it can deliver the site and keep it secure.

03How we use it

We use the information you send us only to:

  • reply to your message and talk with you about your project;
  • prepare quotes, proposals and agreements you ask us for;
  • deliver and support the work you hire us to do;
  • keep records we are required to keep by law, for example for invoicing.

We do not sell your information, and we do not add you to a mailing list without asking you first.

04Cookies and tracking

This website does not set any cookies and does not store anything in your browser's local storage. There is no analytics or ad tracking, so there is no cookie banner to accept.

05Third-party services

  • Google Fonts. One of the typefaces on this site is loaded from Google's servers, which means your browser sends your IP address to Google when the page loads. Google's own privacy policy applies to that request.
  • Email. Our studio inboxes are hosted by Gmail, so the messages you send us are stored by Google.
  • Web3Forms. Messages sent through the contact forms are delivered by Web3Forms, which forwards them to our inbox. Their privacy policy applies to that delivery.
  • External links. This site links to our profiles on LinkedIn, Instagram, X, Behance and GitHub. Those websites have their own privacy policies, and we are not responsible for how they handle your information.

06Sharing your information

Your enquiry is seen only by the daizn studio team members who need it to help you. We share personal information outside the studio only when:

  • you ask us to, or agree to it;
  • a service provider needs it to help us run the studio, such as our email provider, under their own privacy and security commitments;
  • the law requires it, or it is needed to protect our rights or someone's safety.

07How long we keep it

We keep enquiry emails for as long as we need them to reply and to follow up on a possible project, and usually no longer than two years after our last contact. If we work together, we keep project and invoicing records for as long as the law requires. You can ask us to delete your messages at any time (see below).

08Your rights

You can ask us to:

  • tell you what personal information we hold about you and send you a copy;
  • correct information that is wrong or out of date;
  • delete your information, unless we have to keep it by law;
  • stop contacting you.

Email us at the address below and we will reply within 30 days. Depending on where you live, you may also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

09Children

This website is meant for businesses and adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 16. If you think a child has sent us their details, contact us and we will delete them.

10Changes to this policy

We may update this policy if our website or the way we work changes. When we do, we will change the "Last updated" date at the top of this page.

11Contact us

For any question about this policy or your information, email daiznstudio@gmail.com.

Want to see how we work with clients? Read our terms and conditions.
